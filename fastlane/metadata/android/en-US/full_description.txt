Freedom to communicate on your own terms

For individuals and organizations alike — private, secure communication for teams, colleagues, companies, institutions, and more.

Tark delivers fast, private, and secure messaging with high-performance voice and video calling. Built on Matrix—the open standard for real-time communication—Tark ensures reliability, transparency, and long-term trust. The app is maintained at: https://github.com/Arkraft-Technica-Limited/TarkApp
.

Stay connected with colleagues, teammates, and partners through:
• Real-time messaging and crystal-clear video calls
• Public channels for open dialogue
• Private / restricted channels for controlled conversations
• Rich message features: reactions, replies, polls, pinned messages, and more
• Video calls while browsing your chats
• Full interoperability with other Matrix-powered apps like FluffyChat, Cinny, and others

<b>Privacy-first</b>
We don’t track you. We don’t profile you. Unlike Big Tech messengers, Tark never mines your data or watches your conversations.

<b>Own your conversations</b>
Choose where your data lives—whether on a public server (matrix.org and many others) or on your own private server and domain. This choice is what sets Tark apart: your data stays yours. You’re in command, not the product.

<b>Communicate in real time, all the time</b>
Use Tark anywhere. Access your fully synchronized message history across all your devices, including the web—always up to date.

<b>Tark is our next-generation client</b>
Experience Tark: faster, streamlined, and more capable. We’re continuously improving the platform and shipping new features at pace.

The application requires the android.permission.REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES permission to allow installation of applications received as attachments, ensuring seamless access to shared software.

The application also requires the USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT permission to deliver call notifications effectively, even when your device is locked.
